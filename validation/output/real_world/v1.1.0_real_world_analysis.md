## Interpretation rules

- close_to_best: automatic median is within 15% of the fastest valid mode.
- `tiny_absolute_regret`: relative regret is high but the absolute difference is below 1 ms.
- measurable_regret: automatic remains correct but a manual strategy is materially faster.
- large_regret_review_trace: inspect the trace and workload shape; the report does not automatically label variance or policy regret as a scheduler defect.
